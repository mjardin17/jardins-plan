## 3. Platform Configuration & Website Harvesting Engine

### 3.1 Setup Wizard Profiles
The interactive wizard aggregates:
*   **Company Context**: Business name, geographical coordinates, operational hours, email, and phone arrays.
*   **Preference Matrix**: Primary AI providers (Gemini, GPT-4, Claude) and brand primary accent colors.

### 3.2 Importer Mechanics (Human-in-the-Loop Guardrail)
If a website is supplied, the platform initiates a secure content scraper:
1.  **Extraction**: Pulls FAQ headers, services list, company history, and terms.
2.  **State Management**: Holds imported content in a `Pending Review` database state.
3.  **Human-in-the-Loop Validation**: To prevent incorrect or misleading pricing from being presented to consumers, the administrator **must check off** a verification box, ensuring complete human control before ingestion into the live vector store.
